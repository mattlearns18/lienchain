# Cowork Handoff — Phase 12 Patient Disbursement Tracking

**Status:** Ready to execute. One commit.
**Context:** The waterfall already computes "Patient Net Recovery" (the residual when `netAvailable > totalBills` after pro-rata + floor enforcement). What's missing is the operator record-keeping for that disbursement. The patient is paid off-chain by the attorney from the trust account (check or wire) — LienChain doesn't touch the patient's money. Phase 12 adds the record-keeping layer that gives LienChain the audit trail without taking on KYC/custody for retail patients. Mirrors the existing Fiat Receipt pattern from Phase 9 Commit 2 almost exactly.

## Goal

Operator can record patient disbursement against a case after the attorney pays the patient from their trust account. The record persists to the case for audit trail. No on-chain transaction for the patient. Patient disbursement is **not** a settlement precondition — operator can record it before or after on-chain clinic payouts.

Operator-only — does NOT appear in the attorney view of the case (same separation rule as fiat receipt).

## Part A — Data model

New optional field on Case: `patientDisbursement`. Shape mirrors `fiatReceipt`:

```js
{
  amount:      number,       // dollars disbursed to patient; prefills from waterfall's Patient Net Recovery
  disbursedAt: ISO date,
  reference:   string,        // trust account check number, wire ref, or ACH ref
  confirmedBy: string,        // operator name or initials
}
```

Stays `null` / `undefined` until the operator records it. Persists to `lienchain:cases` (existing localStorage key).

## Part B — Settle Now confirmation flow placement

In `AttorneyPreview.jsx`, when the operator opens the Settle Now confirmation modal:

- **If `waterfall.patientNetRecovery > 0`** (overcoverage scenario, common when settlement is large): show a "Patient Net Recovery" line in the confirmation modal's amount breakdown, alongside the existing "To LienCo" and "To Clinic(s)" lines.
- **If `waterfall.patientNetRecovery === 0`** (pool exhausted, exact coverage, or shortfall): omit the patient line entirely — there's nothing to disburse.

Below the amount breakdown, render a status strip + action button gated on `isOperatorView`:

### When `case.patientDisbursement` is null AND patient net > 0

```
⚠ Patient disbursement not yet recorded — $X owed to patient
[ Mark Patient Disbursed ]
```

Amber/yellow styling (not blocking — settlement can still execute). Clicking the button opens the patient disbursement modal (Part C).

### When `case.patientDisbursement` exists

```
✓ Patient disbursed — $X on {date} by {confirmedBy}
Reference: {reference}
[ Update ]
```

Green strip matching the existing fiat receipt strip pattern. Update link re-opens the modal pre-populated.

### When `waterfall.patientNetRecovery === 0`

No strip, no button. Don't surface anything about patient disbursement when there's nothing to disburse.

**Important: do NOT gate Settle Now on patient disbursement.** Clinics get paid on-chain first; the attorney handles patient disbursement on their own timeline. Operator records it whenever the attorney confirms the patient received funds.

## Part C — Mark Patient Disbursed modal

Standard modal styled to match the existing Mark Fiat Received modal (reuse the same `db-form-*` classes from the Phase 8 modal CSS that Mark Fiat Received already uses).

**Fields:**

| Field | Type | Required | Prefill |
|---|---|---|---|
| Amount Disbursed | number ($) | yes | `waterfall.patientNetRecovery` from current waterfall result |
| Date Disbursed | date | yes | Today |
| Reference | text | yes | "" — placeholder: "Trust account check #, wire ref, or ACH ref" |
| Confirmed by | text | yes | "" — placeholder: "Your initials or name" |

**Footer:**

- Cancel (closes without saving)
- Save (writes `case.patientDisbursement`, closes modal, re-renders case view)

**Amount mismatch warning (same pattern as fiat receipt):** if the operator enters an Amount that differs from the prefilled `patientNetRecovery` by more than $1, show a yellow note below the input: `"Amount differs from expected $X by $Y. Confirm before saving."` Non-blocking.

## Part D — Operator-only display rule

Same rule as fiat receipt. The patient disbursement strip + Mark Patient Disbursed button MUST NOT appear in the attorney's view of the case. Gate everything on the existing `isOperatorView` prop that the Dashboard's Attorney View tab passes to `AttorneyPreview`. Direct `/attorney/:caseId` route and `/attorney/demo` both leave the prop defaulting to false, so the patient disbursement UI never appears in attorney views.

This matches the CLAUDE.md §4 operator-vs-attorney separation rule — patient disbursement involves attorney trust account flow that's the attorney's own business; the operator's record-keeping shouldn't bleed into the attorney's portal.

## Part E — Liens tab subtle indicator

On the Liens tab parent row for any settled case where `waterfall.patientNetRecovery > 0`:

- If `case.patientDisbursement` exists: show a small `[ Patient ✓ ]` pill alongside the existing Settled badge
- If `case.patientDisbursement` is null: show a small `[ Patient ⌛ ]` pill (amber) indicating pending operator confirmation

Skip the indicator entirely when patient net was 0 — no signal needed.

Match the existing `db-invite-badge` styling pattern from Phase 8 (Invited / Accepted badges).

## What NOT to touch

- The waterfall math — `waterfall.patientNetRecovery` is already computed correctly; this commit just consumes it.
- The on-chain settlement flow — no patient payment is added.
- The fiat receipt flow — unchanged, parallel record.
- The attorney provisioning / token gate from Phase 8.
- Anything outside the case-level Settle Now confirmation modal and the Liens tab parent row indicator.

## Acceptance criteria

1. **Patient net > 0 scenario.** Open a case with a generous gross settlement (e.g. $50K gross on a $22K total bills case → $13,500 patient net). In Attorney View, the Settle Now confirmation modal shows a "Patient Net Recovery — $13,500.00" line in the amount breakdown. Below the breakdown, an amber strip reads "⚠ Patient disbursement not yet recorded — $13,500 owed to patient" with a "Mark Patient Disbursed" button.

2. **Mark Patient Disbursed modal.** Click the button → modal opens with Amount prefilled to $13,500, Date prefilled to today, Reference + Confirmed by empty. Submit with valid inputs → modal closes, the strip flips to green "✓ Patient disbursed — $13,500.00 on {date} by {initials}".

3. **Persistence.** `case.patientDisbursement` is written to localStorage with all four fields. Reload — strip still shows green.

4. **Update flow.** Click Update → modal re-opens with the saved values prefilled.

5. **Patient net = 0 scenario.** Set gross to a low number so net pool falls short of bills. The Patient Net Recovery line, the strip, and the Mark Patient Disbursed button are all absent. (No signal at all — there's nothing to disburse.)

6. **Settle Now is NOT gated on patient disbursement.** Open a case with patient net > 0 and no `patientDisbursement` recorded. Settle Now is still enabled (assuming fiat receipt is confirmed). The on-chain clinic payouts execute. Patient disbursement remains a pending operator task afterward.

7. **Operator-only display.** Open the same case via `/attorney/:caseId?token=...` (direct attorney route). The Patient Net Recovery line, the strip, the button, and the Liens tab pill all DO NOT appear. The attorney sees only the existing waterfall and clinic information.

8. **Liens tab pill.** For a settled case with patient net > 0 and no patient disbursement recorded: `[ Patient ⌛ ]` amber pill on the parent row. After recording: `[ Patient ✓ ]` green pill. For a settled case with patient net = 0: no patient pill at all.

9. Build clean, no console errors.
